Settings

Academic year runs July–June. "Last 5 years" means the 5 completed academic years before the reference date's academic year; "last 3 years" means the 3 completed ones. Use a past date to reproduce an older report.

Credit rules: sole guide 1; with any co-guide, the guide gets 0.5 and every department co-guide gets 0.5 (external co-guides are recorded but not credited). GC = MAX(1, GC_5, GC_3), with values below 1 rounded up to 1. CAP = ROUND(5×GC − N1).
